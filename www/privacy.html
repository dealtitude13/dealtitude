Dealtitude

Privacy

What we know about you, in plain words. It is less than you would expect.

Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version. If you are a shopper, Dealtitude has no account for you, no name, no email address and no phone number. A shop you follow is never told who its followers are — only how many. We do not sell anything to anyone, and there is no advertising or tracking in this app.

If you are a shopper

You never make an account. When you follow a shop, your phone is given a random identifier, and that is who you are to us. There is nothing attached to it that could identify you.

What is stored

  • A random identifier for your phone, created the first time you follow a shop.
  • A notification address — a token given to us by Apple, Google or your browser so a notification can reach your phone. It is meaningless to anyone else and cannot be used to identify you.
  • Which shops you follow, and which aisles you chose in each one.
  • Which alerts were sent to your phone, and whether you opened them. This is how a shop knows whether its alerts are worth sending. The shop sees totals, never which phone did what.
  • Your browser or device type, which is sent automatically by every app and website and is used to work out whether notifications can reach you at all.

What is not stored

  • Your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your location. We ask for a ZIP code to show you nearby shops; we do not keep it, and we never read your phone's GPS.
  • Your date of birth. See below.
  • Anything about what you buy. Dealtitude is not connected to any till or payment system and has no idea whether you ever walked into the shop.

Age confirmation at shops that sell alcohol

A shop selling alcohol asks you to confirm your age before you can follow it. You enter a month and year of birth.

That date never leaves your phone. Your phone works out whether you meet the age limit and sends us only the answer — that this phone has confirmed being 21 or over. There is no date of birth in our records, because we never receive one.

If you run a shop

Shop owners do have an account, because a shop's alerts must not be postable by strangers.

  • Your email address, used to send you a sign-in link. There is no password.
  • Your shop's details — name, address, opening hours, aisles — which are public, because they appear to shoppers.
  • What you have posted, and how many followers each post reached.

What a shop can see about its followers

Numbers only. How many people follow the shop, how many can receive notifications, how many have each aisle switched on, and how many opened a particular alert.

A shop is never shown who its followers are, and the app has no way to reveal it. There is no list to hand over, because the information does not exist in a form that could identify anyone.

Notifications

Alerts are delivered by Apple, Google or your browser's notification service, depending on your phone. To do that, the alert's text passes through them. We send only what you see on the lock screen: the shop's name and what is on.

You can switch notifications off at any time in your phone's settings, or mute an individual shop inside the app. Muting a shop keeps you following it; you simply stop being notified.

Who else sees any of this

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share your information. There is no advertising network, no analytics company and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.

The service runs on hosting and database providers who store this information on our behalf, and on Apple's and Google's notification services as described above. That is the whole list.

Removing yourself

Unfollowing a shop removes that follow. Deleting the app removes your phone's identifier from our side the next time we try to reach it and find it gone.

If you want everything associated with your phone deleted outright, write to urvik.trivedi@gmail.com and we will do it. Since we hold no name or email for shoppers, you may need to tell us which shops you follow so we can find the right record.

Children

Dealtitude is not directed at children, and shops selling alcohol are gated behind an age confirmation. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the date at the top changes and the change is described here.
