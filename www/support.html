For shoppers

I'm not getting any alerts

Three things to check, in order. First, that notifications are switched on for Dealtitude in your phone's settings. Second, that you have picked at least one aisle in that shop — a new follow starts with every aisle off, so only shop-wide news like a change of hours reaches you until you choose. Third, that you haven't muted the shop.

How do I follow a shop?

Scan the code on the shop's counter, or open the app and enter your ZIP code to see shops near you. Tap Follow, then pick the aisles you actually buy from.

I'm getting too many alerts

Open the shop in the app and switch off aisles you don't care about, or mute the shop entirely — muting keeps you following, you just stop being notified. Every shop also has a daily limit on how many alerts one person can receive from it, so no shop can flood you however much it posts.

Do I need an account?

No. Shoppers never make one. There is nothing to sign up for, no password and no email address to hand over.

Can the shop see who I am?

No. A shop is shown how many people follow it and how many opened an alert, never who. See the privacy page for exactly what is and isn't stored.

I got a new phone

Follows live on the phone, not on an account, so they don't move across by themselves. Follow the shops again on the new phone — scanning the counter code is quickest.

Why is a shop asking my age?

Shops that sell alcohol have to. Your date of birth stays on your phone and is never sent to us; all we record is that the phone confirmed being over 21.

For shops

I can't sign in

Use the email address your invitation was sent to, and check the junk folder. The link works once and lasts 20 minutes — if you've opened it already, or waited, ask for a new one. Shop access only ever comes from an invitation, so if you have not had one, email us.

I posted something and nobody was alerted

The app tells you why on the screen right after you post. The usual reason is that the aisle you posted in isn't one your followers chose. The other common one is a follower whose phone can't receive notifications yet.

How many alerts can I send?

As many as you like. What protects your followers is a daily cap on how many any one person receives from your shop, not a quota on your posting.

Something is wrong on my shop's page

Email us with what needs changing — name, address, hours or aisles — and we'll correct it.

Billing

Billing is handled directly with us and nothing is charged through the app. Email us with any question about your account.

Something else

If none of the above covers it, email urvik.trivedi@gmail.com. Include the shop's name and, if you can, roughly when it happened — it makes it much faster to find.
